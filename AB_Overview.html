Moving around the map

Move the mapClick and drag. On a trackpad, press and drag.
Zoom in or outUse the + and − buttons, pinch on a trackpad, or scroll with a mouse wheel.
See a stationClick a coloured circle. A box opens with its readings. Scroll inside the box if it is long.
Check a spotClick anywhere on the map to see the nearest stations, sensors and weather.
Find a placeType an address or town in the box at the top right and press Go.
Start overPress Clear Selection (bottom left).
Show or hide layersTick the boxes in the list at the top right.

Tip: if the map moves too much on a trackpad, use the + and − buttons to zoom and drag to move.
